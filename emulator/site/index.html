SD card

The board has an SD card on P1.4–P1.7 (SPI). Here it is a 16 MiB FAT16 image stored in your browser. Card files use 8.3 names in the root directory.

Your first card includes dropblk.asm. Existing cards are unchanged until you click Add DropBlocks (FAT16). Download source for other cards or a real board. Insert card… accepts FAT16/FAT32 images up to 256 MiB.
